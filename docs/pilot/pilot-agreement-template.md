## 2. What the pilot is

A limited trial of the Transparent SACCO tool for **recording and approving** the SACCO's deposits, loans and expenses. The program is a record, not a custodian: money keeps moving through mobile money, bank accounts and cash; the tool makes the record tamper-evident and member-visible.

## 3. What the SACCO agrees to

1. **Record deposits** with the treasurer's attestation (type: savings or share capital, amount, reference) and give members a chance to confirm their records.
2. **Run loans through the flow** — apply, committee multi-sig approve, disburse with evidence, record repayments.
3. **Run expenses through multi-sig** — proposal with stated purpose and evidence, approvals, recorded execution with payment proof.
4. **Name the roles**: one admin, at least the required approvers, and at least one Supervisory Committee member (a person who is not an approver).
5. **Set the approval threshold** (how many approvers must approve a loan or expense).
6. **Allow a baseline and end-of-pilot member trust survey** (10 minutes, anonymous).
7. **Allow publication of aggregate results** (no member-identifying data) on the Logos forum.

## 4. What Transparent SACCO agrees to

1. **Train** the treasurer, committee and Supervisory Committee member on the tool.
2. **Support** during the pilot: a named contact and a response time the parties agree on.
3. **Privacy**: member balances stay private per the privacy design; SACCO totals are public.
4. **No cost** to the SACCO for the pilot.
5. **Exit**: either party may end the pilot with a week's notice; the SACCO's data is exported on request.
