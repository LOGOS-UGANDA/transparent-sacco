# Pilot metrics and baseline trust survey

> Phase 0 deliverable: the metrics and the survey instrument, agreed **before** the pilot starts so the before/after comparison is honest. Three metrics, per the roadmap — no more.

## The three metrics

| # | Metric | How we measure it | When |
|---|--------|-------------------|------|
| 1 | **Members who check their record** | Unique member logins during the pilot, against active membership | Weekly |
| 2 | **Share of activity on chain** | Deposits, loans and expenses recorded in the tool, against the paper books for the same days | Weekly spot-check |
| 3 | **Member trust** | The survey below, run before and after the pilot | Baseline + end |

## Baseline trust survey (run before the pilot)

Anonymous, 10 questions, paper or phone. Say out loud before starting: *this is not a test, answers do not affect anyone's savings, and nobody sees individual answers.*

Rate 1–5 (1 = strongly disagree, 5 = strongly agree):

1. I trust that my savings balance is recorded correctly.
2. I know how the SACCO decides who gets a loan.
3. I know how SACCO money is spent on expenses.
4. I believe expenses are spent for the stated purpose.
5. I would notice if my record was wrong.
6. I know who to ask when something looks wrong.
7. I trust the committee to follow the SACCO's rules.
8. I would report a problem with my record if I saw one.
9. I understand what my share capital is (vs my savings).
10. I would recommend this SACCO to a family member.

**Score:** average of all answers (1–5). Report the average and the answer distribution — not individual sheets.

## After the pilot

Run the identical survey. Report both scores next to metrics 1 and 2 in the public result post (Logos forum): what was recorded, how many members checked, and whether trust moved.
